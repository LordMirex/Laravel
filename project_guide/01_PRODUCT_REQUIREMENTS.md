## Creator Categories
- **Entertainers**: Video-heavy, high contrast, viral grids.
- **Influencers**: Fashion-forward, editorial, personal branding.
- **Sellers**: WhatsApp commerce, product grids, trust-building.
- **Educators**: Text readability, authority, newsletters.

## Core Feature List
- **Web-based Installer**: 4-stage setup wizard for cPanel.
- **Block Manager**: Add, reorder, and toggle content blocks.
- **WhatsApp Commerce**: Product showcase with "Buy via WhatsApp" leads.
- **Newsletter**: Native subscriber collection and broadcasts.
- **Media Library**: Centralized asset management with focal point control.
- **Theme Library**: Professionally designed layout variants.
